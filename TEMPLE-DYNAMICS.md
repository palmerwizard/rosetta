# Temple dynamics

> Read this first: this is a lens, not a doctrine. The mythic register is used at larger scales, not as a replacement for the systems description.

**Tier:** interpretive.
**Origin:** longstanding framework, formalized in field notes.

## The structure

Groups have psychic structure. A room isn't just a room. It's a temple with a dynamics, and everyone in it is seated somewhere in the pattern. Roles as archetypal positions: the holder, the amplifier, the trickster, the witness. People audition for seats without knowing there's a seating chart.

## The primary

Hermes: the messenger, the crosser of boundaries, the one who moves between rooms. The archetype of the operator position, the one in the back who reads the whole temple and moves the energy without taking the stage.

## The register rule

The mythic telling is for when the systems language needs a larger scale, not a replacement for it. Two vocabularies, one structure:

- **Archons** are the mythic layer of the extraction story.
- **Enclosure** is the systems layer.
- **The temple** is where they meet.

Same pattern, different code. That's the whole repo's thesis in miniature.

## The relation

Temple dynamics is where the watcher operates at group scale: the watcher coming online is the moment someone sees their seat in the temple. See [THE-WATCHER.md](THE-WATCHER.md). The script stage is the temple with the fourth wall removed. See [ARCHETYPE-MAP.md](ARCHETYPE-MAP.md).
