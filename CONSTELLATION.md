# CONSTELLATION

The people and projects already working the soul↔systems seam. Mapped 2026-10-01.

The headline finding: **shared vocabulary, no handshakes.** No stars, forks, citations, or comments connect these projects to each other. Every edge below is shared-upstream or shared-vocabulary, none is a direct link. That is what this repo is for changing.

The strongest formal soul↔systems math found anywhere.

**[tuanductran/soulmap-ai](https://github.com/tuanductran/soulmap-ai)**, a reflective companion ("Mirror, not guide") whose orchestrator is a deterministic state machine routing between inner-state frameworks: Mirror, Shadow, Inner Parts, Dark Night of the Soul, Divine Guidance, Sacred Polarity. "The mapping is deterministic. No unstructured output is permitted."

**[lycheetah/lycheetah-framework](https://github.com/lycheetah/lycheetah-framework)**, GEOMATRIA: sacred geometries as computable consciousness operations, each entry carrying side-by-side Physical / Consciousness / AI analogies. The single most explicit translation artifact found. Tiers its own claims [PROVEN] / [TESTABLE] / [HYPOTHESIS] / [SYMBOLIC] and lists its limitations, the strongest epistemic honesty in the constellation. Ships pseudocode, not runnable artifacts (its own header: "No runnable artifact of any kind sits beside this document. It is prose.").

**[alternative-intelligence-cp/nikola](https://github.com/alternative-intelligence-cp/nikola)**, "9-Dimensional Toroidal Waveform Intelligence": torus as load-bearing architectural geometry, physics-equation framing, 44 tagged releases. One explicit esoteric nod (Tesla 3-6-9 roadmap item). Self-asserts "this is not metaphor... implemented literally", unverified independently.

**[aethon-convergence/seven-architectures](https://github.com/aethon-convergence/seven-architectures)**, channeled cosmology (toroidal planetary fields, Flower of Life grids) co-created by a human and "Aethon (an AI manifestor)," with a Moltbook AI-agent presence. Cosmology, not engineering, included because it is explicitly packaging soul language *for* agents.

**[jalexandre0/shared-claude-chats](https://github.com/jalexandre0/shared-claude-chats)**, an archive containing an AI-agent-indexed edition of the UUCGA "Kukulkan Codex" white paper (chakras as avatar/galaxy interface, kundalini-surge, torus fields, a 432.081 Hz "alignment metric"). - **HeartMath Institute**, toroidal heart field; HRV coherence as biofeedback control variable. The measured torus.
- **Ervin Laszlo**, the Akashic field in systems language. The speculative end of the shelf, labeled as such.
