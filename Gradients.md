# Gradients Draft — The Field of Gradients

**CANDIDATE DRAFT. Nothing here is settled.** Filed 2026-10-26.

**These are the gradients which the system extracts from to prevent the human (self) from achieving the steering position and having greater steering power.**

**The frame:** steering is the pattern, performance is the byproduct, the use is the question. The steerers lay the gradient; the gradient persists even when steerers change; some are wired to feed into it. The board is a field of gradients laid by different steerers, people absorbed by whichever pull matches their wiring.
**The gradient, defined:** a slope in the incentive landscape — pressure difference + local steps only + no author + coordination-shaped output. A pattern that moves through the environment and shapes behavior without anyone authoring it.
**Symbols, double-loaded by design:** symbols = (1) literal glyphs, the substrate, AND (2) meaning-carrying symbols used as weapons. The symbol is the medium every gradient runs through.

## 1. Money/status gradient

- **Pulls toward:** money and status, whoever sits uphill on them.
- **Who lays it:** steerers — the gradient persists even when steerers change.
- **Who gets absorbed:** the gradient-wired — people who follow the gradient, not surrender to it.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** the power of attorney (legal paper that hands over the wheel), the managed social account (a name as a handle, written by another), the licensed image, the synthetic version, the profit share.
- **Source:** theories.md entry 4.

## 2. Attention gradient

- **Pulls toward:** the feed, the next thing, staying without ever leaving full.
- **Who lays it:** the platform metric — no author. The authorless operation: maximize engagement → confession → targeting → steering.
- **Who gets absorbed:** everyone in the room where people want to be seen; attention kept flowing by engineered friction, just enough novelty to stay.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** the like, the follower count, the impression, the engagement metric — counts that rise while the function (verifiable information, local reporting) collapses.
- **Source:** extraction map v8 ("The authorless operation"); ontology Gradient class.

## 3. Confession gradient

- **Pulls toward:** admitting, volunteering the material — preference, position, secrets.
- **Who lays it:** rooms built for volunteering — the feed rewards confession with visibility and files everything.
- **Who gets absorbed:** people who want to be seen; the strategic confessor buys trust cheap, the costly confessor pays to be known.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** the like button (a confession of preference), the comment box (a confession of position), the DM (a confession with privacy still attached); the cheap/costly split prices every admission.
- **Source:** extraction map v8 ("The authorless operation," "What gets extracted"); confession-cluster-table.md.

## 4. Legitimacy gradient (credentials)

- **Pulls toward:** the credential, the grade, the score — the symbol that opens the door.
- **Who lays it:** whoever holds the pen and the printer — the issuer. The decoupled symbol set: the symbol keeps circulating while the referent is gone.
- **Who gets absorbed:** everyone downstream who prices in the fake set — wages, savings, contracts all compound the spread silently.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** GPA and degrees (rise while measured skills fall), credit scores and assets (multiply while productive lending shrinks), filings and plea counts (rise while the trial vanishes), billing codes and RVUs (expand while outcomes stall), the AAA rating (the 2008 case: symbol and function diverged catastrophically at once), elections and turnout (stay high while trust falls to 27–28%).
- **Source:** systems-collapse-chart/research.md (symbol vs function, all 13 systems); extraction map v8 ("The decoupled symbol set").

## 5. Belonging gradient

- **Pulls toward:** the group, the room, the people — being inside instead of outside.
- **Who lays it:** the platform (parasocial highway) and the scene — the room where the connection is supposed to happen.
- **Who gets absorbed:** the lonely — the pull is toward anyone who seems to offer the circle.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** "friends," followers, and group memberships online — counts that rise while in-person time, confidants, and civic organizations thin out.
- **Source:** systems-collapse-chart/research.md (community).

## 6. Outrage/blame gradient

- **Who gets absorbed:** reward circuits that pay out on blame, control, and status; duper's delight pays the deceiver now and charges the deceived later.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** the accusation, the liar label (the reporter is the problem — deferred maintenance's denial instrument), the category word that discharges the map ("philosophy, existentialism, theory" — comfort compliance); the move that relocates the malfunction into the witness.

## 7. Desire/feeling gradient (siren song)

- **Pulls toward:** the feeling itself — the hit, the frisson, the manufactured reward.
- **Who lays it:** whoever can model the receiver — generated tracks and art now; the alarm rings when a generated track moves a room, not a blind demo.
- **Who gets absorbed:** the listener whose feeling gets printed — the receiver is being modeled well enough to play, and a modeled receiver is a tapped receiver.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** the track, the stream, the retention metric — the industry prices the trace while the feeling stays off the balance sheet; the counter: what can't be formulaized can't be automated, the feeling was never in the signal; frisson is symbol creating nervous system feedback; taste is the discriminator — the siren song only works if it can sing your song.

## 8. Time gradient (chronodemon)

- **Pulls toward:** exhaustion, compliance that needs no enforcing — tired people scroll, they don't revolt.
- **Who lays it:** the schedule — the job highway. Work that distributes the hours across every day strips the watcher; work that concentrates the hours and leaves days empty preserves it.
- **Who gets absorbed:** the unslacked — an entire generation priced out of the time to think; no hours, no community, no seeing, no compounding.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** the timesheet, the shift, the schedule — time is the one raw material that cannot be faked, borrowed, or regenerated, which is why it is stripped first; the fringe's name for it is the chronodemon, and the demon runs payroll.
- **Source:** extraction map v8 ("Temporal displacement").

## 9. Over-giving gradient (care)

- **Pulls toward:** giving more — labor, care, boundarylessness, the extra hour, the free favor.
- **Who lays it:** systems that benefit from over-giving and punish the boundary — the punishment is structural, not personal.
- **Who gets absorbed:** the people least able to price it — empaths pay the hidden boundary tax to integrate; free favors quietly become the standardized expectation.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** the favor, the ask, the "no" that gets punished; the boundary itself is the symbol under attack — every boundary set against extraction gets taxed.
- **Source:** extraction map v8 ("What gets extracted: Over-giving"); coinage ledger (hidden boundary tax, boundary tax).

## 10. Altitude gradient (control layer)

- **Pulls toward:** the floor above — higher, untouchable, out of jurisdiction.
- **Who lays it:** whoever builds the next floor — the space race, the satellite layer; whoever holds the sky holds what counts as observed on the ground.
- **Who gets absorbed:** the masses get the consumption layer (fancy tools that eat the hours the watcher needs); the builders get the control layer (fancier tools that buy the next floor up). Altitude compounds — from the floor above you can see the whole board, so the next floor up is cheaper to reach.
- **Symbols that manipulate it:** the satellite, the rocket, the floor above the law — one layer goes transparent, extraction migrates to a floor nobody is watching yet.
- **Source:** theories.md entry 1; extraction map v8 (move 2, "Move up a layer").

## How symbols manipulate gradients (the mechanism)

- **Symbols set the slope.** The token layer (scores, counts, grades, follower numbers) points the pull — the slope runs toward whatever the symbol counts. Change the counted symbol and the gradient re-aims.
- **Symbols hide the slope.** The decoupled symbol set circulates while the referent is gone, so the gradient can't be audited — ask what the symbol is anchored to; if the answer is an appeal to trust rather than a checkable anchor, the anchor is the issuer.
- **Symbols pre-load the language.** Vocabulary planted upstream of thought steers every conclusion later drawn in it — the thinking arrives pre-steered, no operation required at the point of decision.
- **Symbols name the destination.** The "special" reframe renames the void as a gift, so the steering looks like care; the credential performs the thinking so the person doesn't have to; the category word discharges the map without touching it.
- **Symbols perform the operation they name.** Σ gathers, Δ divides; the symbol lands and the body answers — frisson is symbol creating nervous system feedback.
- **The counter:** re-anchor or exit — a counter-ledger priced in the territory (hours, matter, outcomes) exposes the spread; the body is the checkable instrument; starve the gradient, price the confession, keep the ledger.
