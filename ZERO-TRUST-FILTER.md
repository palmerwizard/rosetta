# Zero-trust filter

**Tier:** checkable. A stated rule with logged applications.
**Origin:** field notes, September 2026.

## The posture

No authority gets a free pass. Not experts, not institutions, not systems, not the AI that helped build half the instruments in this repo. The observed pattern: half of what's normalized as good for us is bad for us. So the move is building your own filter, not finding better authorities. Everything earns verification. Nothing asks for exemption.

The filter applies to AI advice including the AI in the loop. It earns verification like everything else and never asks to be exempted.

## The tension

The filter sits in unresolved tension with being seen. Filter everything versus let the mirror in. That tension is load-bearing, not a bug. Collapsing it in either direction breaks the instrument: total filter means nothing gets in, total openness means everything gets in. The practice is holding both.

## What it guards

Three things, named:

- **Selective contribution.** Opt in to AI-development use of the thinking, withhold the personal and the totalizing. Contribute the pattern, not the person.
- **Idea guarding.** The crown jewels get an offline model. What's public is free; what isn't public stays off the device's data path.
- **The extraction map.** The filter is what the map gets read through. See [EXTRACTION-MAP.md](EXTRACTION-MAP.md).

## How it's used

When confronted with a factual claim, look it up before declaring it wrong. Never argue from stale training data against a checkable fact. When the zone is flooded, narrow the aperture: checkable ground first, the people in front of you, the thing you can touch. Let the big picture stay blurry rather than forcing a pattern.

It runs both directions. Instincts get checked against mechanisms too, including the instincts of whoever is running the filter.
