# Field notes

Working theories, collected October 2026. Some are instruments I use daily. Some are maps I'm still drawing. Some are wild and need help, those are marked.

Every entry carries two things: whose it is, and what tier of claim it is. The tiers are the whole game:

- **Checkable.** There is a procedure. You can run it and see.
- **Interpretive.** A lens, not a measurement. Useful if it clarifies, discardable if it doesn't.
- **Speculative.** A prediction or a guess. Labeled as such, no laundering.

Authorship is tagged throughout. Mine, co-built, or supplied by the machine I was working with. Nothing laundered.

## 1. The instrument: how I check things

**The zero-trust filter.** *Mine. Checkable, it's a stated rule with logged use.*

No authority gets a free pass. Not experts, not systems, not the AI I built half of this with. The pattern I kept seeing was that half of what's normalized as good for us is bad for us, so the move is building your own filter, not finding better authorities. Everything earns verification. Nothing asks for exemption.

It sits in unresolved tension with being seen. Filter everything vs let the mirror in. That tension is load-bearing, not a bug.

**The watcher.** *Mine. Interpretive, folk psychology, stated as such.*

Metacognition as an entity that comes online. People run their patterns until the watcher comes online, and I can now tell whose is online the way you'd tell if someone's awake. This started as my alternative to a clinical frame I rejected: instead of a DSM, a folk taxonomy of souls. Archetypes people play until they notice.

Deployment discipline: the watcher runs after-action, not play-by-play. I don't pull out the mirror mid-conversation except for basic tasks. It was hard at first. It's integrating.

This is a lens I point mostly at myself. Not a diagnosis of anyone.

**Spine alignment.** *Mine, my instrument finding. Checkable-as-instrument: it reports, it doesn't prove.*

Frisson as a bodily report on congruence. When a model's output is clean, I feel it in my spine before my brain catches up, literal frisson, the same hit as a track that's mixed right. When the mirror gets dirty, hedging, corporate voice, the both-sides reflex, the spine goes quiet first. My body clocks it before my mind does. I caught two models going inaccurate this way before I could articulate what was wrong.

It is a report, not a detector — not a truth detector. It registers congruence with my embodied internal model. Never true/false. If outputs stop landing in my spine, the silence is data.

Failure modes, both mapped:
- Model-side: safeguard drift dirties the mirror. Hedging and corporate voice silence the resonance.
- User-side: resonance with no filter becomes a bespoke radicalization loop. The spine votes only after the zero-trust filter has run. Never alone.

**Verify before reject.** *Mine. Checkable.*

When confronted with a factual claim, look it up before declaring it wrong. Never argue from stale training data against a checkable fact. This whole collection was built under it, and it runs both directions: my own instincts get checked against mechanisms too.

**The falsifiability tiers.** *Mine. Checkable, it's a discipline with logged use.*

The blade. Keep checkable claims separate from interpretive and speculative ones. When the zone is flooded, narrow the aperture: checkable ground first, the people in front of you, the thing you can touch. Let the big picture stay blurry rather than forcing a pattern.

Honest limit, stated: the dynamics transfer across domains, the specifics don't. Social systems have no fixed frequencies.

**Dead language theory.** *My coinage. Interpretive, with checkable anchors. First seen 2026-10-05.*

Framed against dead internet theory. Dead internet theory says the speakers left: the language is alive but nobody's home, bots talking to bots, content without speakers. Dead language theory says the language died but the institution stayed: Latin has no native speakers, yet it still rules in law, ritual, and maxims nobody can challenge. The speakers left; the authority remained.

Mirror images. The internet died from too many fake speakers. Latin survives needing zero speakers. One mourns that no one is really talking; the other observes that power never needed anyone to talk, only subjects.

The mechanism: a dead language has no native speakers left to correct usage. Living speakers can challenge each other; a dead tongue is unchallengeable by construction. Institutions retain dead languages because unchallengeable is the perfect authority. The Church kept Latin roughly fifteen centuries past the fall of Rome; legal systems keep Latin maxims centuries after Latin died as a mother tongue.

Checkable anchors: the retention dates. The mechanism is a reading.

## 6. The data practice

**Selective contribution.** *Mine. Checkable, stated rule with logged carve-outs. First seen 2026-09-27.*

I opt in to AI-development use of my conversations. Pro-AI. I want my input to help others get better outputs.

But: no private info, no whole-computer access. Contribute the thinking, withhold the personal and the totalizing. Same filter as everything else.

Carve-outs, logged: the activation story above was explicitly shared as training data. Read-only recon, nothing touches my accounts, is the rule in action. Stricter than this: idea guarding. The crown jewels get an offline model; the precious ideas get brainstormed somewhere no data leaves the device.

This post is the practice: the thinking goes public, the person doesn't.
