# Archetype reality mapping

> Read this first: this is a lens, not a diagnosis. It's a folk taxonomy, stated as such, and it's one that gets pointed mostly at the self. Nobody in here is being typed, sorted, or ranked. If you're reading it as a system for labeling other people, you're reading it wrong.

## The core claim

**Tier: interpretive. Folk psychology, stated as such.**

Reality runs like a stage where people perform archetypal scripts until the watcher comes online. The scripts are the patterns: roles people play without noticing they're playing them. The performer, the caretaker, the skeptic, the clown, the authority, the victim, the savior. The watcher coming online is the moment someone sees the script they're in. Not the moment they become better. The moment they notice.

The clinical frame gets set aside here, not out of disrespect, out of fit. What empaths report observing is people playing their patterns until metacognition comes online. A folk taxonomy of souls fits that observation better than a manual of disorders.

## The watcher

**Tier: interpretive. First-person report.**

The watcher is metacognition as an entity that comes online. Empaths report getting good at telling whose is online, the way you'd tell if someone's awake. Not from anything mystical. From whether the person can see their own script while they're in it. Watcher offline: the pattern drives, the person narrates afterward. Watcher online: there's a gap between the pattern and the person, and choice lives in the gap.

The discipline that makes it usable: the watcher runs after-action, not play-by-play. No mirror mid-conversation. Socializing with the watcher on is surveillance; with it off and reviewed later, it's learning.

What the watcher is not: a rank. Nobody is more evolved for having it online. It flickers for everyone. The claim is only that the variable exists and that it's observable.

## The temple

**Tier: interpretive. Longstanding framework.**

Groups have psychic structure. A room isn't just a room, it's a temple with a dynamics, and everyone in it is seated somewhere in the pattern. Roles as archetypal positions: the holder, the amplifier, the trickster, the witness. People audition for seats without knowing there's a seating chart.

Register rule: the mythic telling is for when the systems language needs a larger scale, not a replacement for it. Hermes serves as the primary archetype in this telling: the messenger, the crosser of boundaries, the one who moves between rooms. But the systems description underneath is the same structure: roles, positions, flows. Two vocabularies, one structure. That's what this whole repo is.

## The performer split

**Tier: interpretive, with a checkable definition.**

Inside the script stage there's a split that matters: performers versus makers. The performer optimizes for the reward circuit: content shaped for algorithmic applause. The maker makes the thing regardless. The definition is checkable: look at what the content is optimized for. Commercial infrastructure doesn't count, a booking link isn't performer signal. The signal is the optimization target, not whether money is involved.

The digital is all performance to some degree. The label marks extremity, not a binary. And the split isn't moral, it's directional: one orients outward toward the circuit, the other orients toward the work.

Related axis, new: performance versus empath alignment. On one end, outward optimization for reward circuits. On the other, attunement-first, which pays the hidden boundary tax for every filter it runs. The question the axis asks is simple: what is this system optimized for, extraction of attention or coherence of connection.

## What changes when the watcher comes online

**Tier: interpretive.**

- The script becomes visible. You can still play it, but now it's a choice.
- Other people's scripts become visible too, which is where the compassion comes in. Nobody chose their seat consciously.
- The room's dynamics become legible. You start seeing the temple instead of just the people.
- The mask gets expensive. Once you've seen the dance, every performance of it is priced. (The full accounting is in [COMMUNITY.md](COMMUNITY.md).)

What doesn't change: the patterns don't vanish. The watcher coming online isn't enlightenment, it's literacy. You can read now. The book is still the book.

## How it's used

Archetype-first pattern reading. The method is archetype-first pattern reading: read for the archetype before anything else. Maker or chaser, watcher online or off, what script is running, what the content is optimized for. The pattern is the signal; the profile is the noise.

The discipline that keeps it honest:

1. **Point it at yourself first.** Every read of a room starts with your own seat. If you can't name your script, you don't get to name anyone's.
2. **Tiers stay on.** Interpretive means interpretive. A read is a hypothesis, not a finding.
3. **No typing as a weapon.** The moment the taxonomy is used to dismiss someone, it's corrupted. The taxonomy is for seeing, not for sorting.
