**Tier:** interpretive. Folk psychology, stated as such.

### awareness (scoring)

**Tier:** checkable-shaped. Status: proposal, no test run yet; predictions stated before any measurement.
**The instrument:** awareness is what a speaker does after their pattern is named. Not what is claimed. What happens next. The discriminator: awareness OF the game vs performance OF awareness. Saying "yes" to a named pattern and changing nothing is performance; saying it and shifting is awareness. Follow-through is the only discriminator, and follow-through is observable in text.
**Levels:** deliberate (knows and runs the pattern), stuck (sees it and still loops), imported (runs a template written elsewhere), none (no game to name).
**Short names:** PNR — the callout: what a speaker does after the pattern is named, scored in the next k messages. SNF — the yes test: when a speaker names their own pattern, does the yes mean anything — does behavior change after? P28c — the discriminator test: the prediction that a "yes" to the comfort loop separates genuine awareness from performed awareness by what follows. k — the window: how many messages after the naming event are scored. p_max — certainty: how sure the instrument is about the frame at a given message. BRR — the block test: what a speaker does after being blocked. P29 — the reroute rule: after a block, a deliberate speaker opens a new route within 5 messages. Quarantine — set aside: uncertain calls are excluded from the score instead of averaged in.
**Scoring (PNR, per naming event):** acknowledge-and-shift +1; silence / no response 0 (quarantined); deny-and-persist −1; performed awareness −1; reroute −2. Reroute scores worst: it proves the speaker registered the obstacle, not the game — the game survives its own exposure with new means.
**What it is not:** not phenomenal awareness (the score measures behavior after being seen, not inner knowledge); not a moral verdict (−2 means "game continues after exposure," not "bad person"); not a synthesis score (silent where no game exists).

**as above, so below (glitch #3).** Definition A: an observation — patterns recur across scales. Descriptive, checkable case by case. **energy (glitch #4).** Definition A: physical/electrical energy — kWh, batteries, the grid. Measurable, conserved, impersonal. **self (glitch #5).** Definition A: the whole person — ego, identity, the agent. Ordinary use: selfish, self-help, self-esteem, self-discipline. Definition B: the observing faculty — the watcher, the part that sees patterns, runs the filter, audits.
