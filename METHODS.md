# METHODS

How to read this repo without fooling yourself.

## The tiers

Every glossary entry carries one:

- **checkable**, the claim can be verified against a source, a text, or running code. ("Bateson wrote 'mind is synonymous with cybernetic system.'" Open the book, there it is.)
- **interpretive**, a genuine reading, grounded in evidence, that reasonable people could dispute. Most of the glossary lives here. That is fine; it is labeled.
- **speculative**, a reach. Included when the reach is interesting, marked so nobody mistakes it for a result.

When flooded, narrow the aperture: start with the checkable entries and earn your way outward.

## The rules

1. **Verify before rejecting.** A claim you dislike still gets looked up before it gets dismissed. The filter is an instrument, not a mood.
2. **Authorship stays clean.** Who said it, who built it, who verified it, credited every time. Ideas do not float free of their makers here.
3. **The map is not the territory.** "Shadow behaves like negative feedback" is a bridge, not an identity proof. Bridges get walked, then inspected.
4. **No borrowed authority.** A pairing is only as strong as its mechanism. "Both involve circles" is not a mechanism.
5. **Interesting is not true.** The speculative tier exists because some reaches are worth keeping visible. They stay in their tier until they earn promotion.

## Verification levels for sources

- **page-read**, someone opened the source and read it (date noted)
- **index**, corroborated across multiple search-index sources, primary text not yet read

These came from a research pass on 2026-10-01. Promote index-level entries to page-read whenever you do the reading.
