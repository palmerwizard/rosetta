The connections nobody has made yet. Each is an open invitation, claim one by making it.

## 1. The clinicians and the builders have never met

The Jungian-complexity literature (van Eenwyk, Tresan, Hogenson, Cambray) published the archetype↔attractor and synchronicity↔emergence bridges between 1991 and 2002. The GitHub builders (prithvi, bluewave, soulmap-ai) implemented shadow↔coefficient and consciousness↔state-machine between 2025 and 2026. As of 2026-10-01, neither side cites, stars, forks, or appears aware of the other. **The tie:** put Cambray on bluewave's reading list and bluewave's ODEs on the clinicians' desk. Someone who has read both should write the synthesis.

## 2. The torus people and the cybernetics people are describing the same topology

HeartMath measures a toroidal heart field. Haramein's lineage theorizes nested toroidal coupling at all scales. lycheetah computes `ToroidalFlow(state_history)`. nikola builds on toroidal waveforms. Von Foerster modeled cognition's double closure *as a torus* decades earlier, the observer closing the loop on itself, topologically a donut. Five groups, one shape, zero shared rooms. **The tie:** a comparative piece tracing the torus from second-order cybernetics through biofeedback to the agent builders, marking at each step what is measured, what is computed, and what is asserted.

## 3. The lived-instrument seat is empty

Almost everything on the seam is third-person theory or third-person instrumentation. Varela, a contemplative practitioner who fused first-person discipline with dynamical-systems neuroscience, is the ancestor, and he died in 2001. One correction to the framing: the mapping is being done. The instruments in this repo are built from a lived practice, mapped the same way. What is not being done is publishing the lived layer itself. That stays off the public record by design: contribute the pattern, not the person. prithvi is the nearest published attempt, and it is a synthetic first person. **The tie:** the seat is occupied, not empty, but the occupant is unnamed. The reference implementation exists. The diarist does not publish.

## 4. The frequency claims need tiers before they can be ties

432.081 Hz "alignment metrics," Tesla 3-6-9 tuning, phi-threshold constants. None of it is currently tie-able because none of it carries a tier: derived, conventional, calibrated, hypothesized, or illustrative. **The tie:** take any one frequency claim, label its constants honestly, and design the experiment that would promote or demote it. lycheetah's ledger already asks for this.
