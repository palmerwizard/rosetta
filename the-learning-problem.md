# The Learning Problem

## The example

On 2026-10-15, CBS News reported that American students' reading scores continue to plummet, with math falling severely. The numbers are the quantitative shadow of a qualitative decay: the faculty rotted in practice first. Scores are just the part that can be put on a scale.

## Mechanism 1: The "good enough to pass" trap

Students get trapped in model loops. The model is prompted, the output is good enough to pass, so the human never learns and takes the easiest route. Prompt, pass, repeat. Capability flatlines.

Why it stabilizes: "good enough to pass" is the trap's threshold. If the output were bad, the friction would force learning. But passable output clears the bar with zero effort, so the loop never demands more. The easiest route wins every round.

The inversion: same machine, opposite effect. Used as an oracle (answer-dispenser), it atrophies. Used as a mirror (load-bearing judgment stays human), it sharpens. The machine is not the variable. The system design is.

## Mechanism 2: Stalling education

Kids don't learn to compute functions:

- **abstraction** (concept formation: extracting general principles from instances)
- **quantitative reasoning** (numeracy: measurement, proportion, comparison)
- **convergent thinking / judgment** (Guilford's convergent production: narrowing multiple inputs to a single verdict; decision-making under uncertainty)

The system teaches passing, not thinking. The functions never get built.

How it runs: same threshold as Mechanism 1. "Good enough to pass" clears the bar, so the functions are never demanded. No demand, no development. The stall is structural, not a failure of effort.

The functions were partly school-taught — algebra builds abstraction and quantitative reasoning for some. No reason to claim it doesn't transfer. The rest developed through experience: the social, the learning, lived friction. Now the social is boxed — isolated feeds, filtered bubbles, little social boxes — and AI fills the learning gap with passable output. The school channel still runs its part; the experience channel is the one that's blocked.

What it feeds: a population missing abstraction, quantitative reasoning, and convergent judgment can't detect extraction. Scripts stay invisible to minds never taught to read code.

The pair: Mechanism 1 is the atrophy (capability decays through the model loop). Mechanism 2 is the stall (capability never built). The system both prevents the muscle from forming and dissolves the muscle that exists.

## Solutions

**Adults: anti-deskilling.** Keep judgment load-bearing on the human. Use the model as a mirror, not an oracle: generate wide, verdict everything, never accept output unchallenged. Friction where thinking happens, flow where mechanics happen. (See THEORIES.md: the mirror material.)

**Children: unknown.** No verified solution — the scale is complete early education reform, unthinkable at individual scale. The functions were never built, so there is nothing to preserve. The problem is construction, not maintenance.
