# Memetic harmonics

**Tier:** interpretive.
**Origin:** co-coined in field notes, October 2026.

## The pattern

Real patterns rhyme through distorted channels: memes, fringe language, official taglines, garbled retellings. The rhyme survives the distortion. What identifies it as a true rhyme rather than noise is the harmonics.

Same math as music: the fundamental carries through distortion, but the overtone pattern says whether it's the same instrument. A true pattern and a coincidental resemblance can share the fundamental. They don't share the overtones.

## The use

Pattern verification across noisy channels. When a pattern seems to rhyme through a distorted medium, check the harmonics: the secondary and tertiary details, the things a surface imitation wouldn't bother to copy. If the harmonics match, it's the same pattern wearing different clothes. If they don't, it's noise wearing the pattern's clothes.

## The relation

A special case of the harmonic structure of systems: feedback loop as resonance, reinforcing and balancing loops as constructive and destructive interference, cross-scale patterns as harmonic series, dissonance resolving as equilibrium. Memetic harmonics is that math applied to verification. Honest limit carries over: the dynamics transfer, the notes don't. Social systems have no fixed frequencies.

## Open edge

The procedure is qualitative. A quantitative version, actual harmonic analysis of pattern transmission, is unbuilt. If the overtones can be formalized, the instrument gets sharper.
