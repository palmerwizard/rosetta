# The Boundary Circuit

*A system theory of boundaries, currents, gatekeepers, and harvests. Claims are tiered: [empirical] = established record, [structural] = the mapping itself, [open] = conjecture held lightly.*

## The claim

One circuit repeats at every scale: a **boundary** holds a **gradient**; a **current** probes the boundary; a **gatekeeper** at the boundary sorts what passes and keeps a **record** of the sorting; something **harvests the difference** the gradient makes possible; the waste is exported to a **sink**. The interesting activity — structure, life, pattern, profit — happens at the interface, never in the bulk on either side.

## The components

- **Boundary.** The given pattern: setpoint, fence, assigned lot. Not chosen by the participant; arrived with. In the contemplative vocabulary this is the *daimon*: the pattern-keeper of a life, amoral about comfort, loyal to the pattern.
- **Current.** The energy in motion inside the boundary, seeking its route, probing the fence. Direction-neutral: steered, it is power under control; unsteered or unbounded, it is flood and weather. The old name for it in the body traditions is the serpent: the coiled current at the base that rises through a channel.
- **Gatekeeper.** The boundary's enforcement arm: a worker at the one door, deciding pass or turn-back, one unit at a time, invisible from the bulk. Physics named him first: Maxwell's demon (1867), the sorting being at the door between two chambers of gas.
- **Record.** The gatekeeper's memory of every sorting decision. Finite. Information is physical: records take room, and clearing room is taxed. [empirical: Landauer 1961; Bennett 1982 — erasing one bit costs a minimum of ~3×10⁻²¹ joules at room temperature; laboratory information-engines in the 2010s converted information to work at the predicted rates.]
- **Gradient.** The difference the boundary maintains: hot/cold, inside/outside, peak/street, congruent/fake. No boundary, no gradient; no gradient, nothing to steer and nothing to steal.
- **Load.** Whatever lives off the difference. In social systems the harvest is often *loosh*: the emotional and attentional energy generated by people held against a boundary, extracted by whoever maintains the gradient.
- **Sink.** Where harvested difference goes to stop circulating. In physics the terminal sink, the black hole, is also where the boundary-information question was settled: a hole's entropy is the bits written on its surface, and the holographic principle — a volume's information encoded on its boundary — fell out of that fight. [empirical]
- **Operator.** The only free position in the circuit for a participant. The boundary is given and the current runs regardless. What can be owned is channel quality and aim: gain and route. Steering, not obedience and not escape.

## The dynamics

1. **The gatekeeper cannot win.** Sorting saves exactly what erasing the record costs. Every extraction gate leaks its profit into record-keeping: the more flow it sorts, the more memory it burns. Fences are maintenance contracts that grow with traffic. [empirical in physics; structural as a social law]
2. **Unprobed boundaries become prisons.** A fence never tested is eventually indistinguishable from a cage, and nobody remembers its purpose. The current's probing is the boundary's audit.
3. **Unbounded currents become weather.** A flow with no banks dissipates or floods. It never becomes a self, a work, or a river.
4. **The harvest is the difference, not the substance.** A venue sells the gradient (peak room vs. street), not the people. A platform sells the outrage gradient. A gift economy is the same circuit with the load wired to circulate the difference back into the room instead of sinking it.
5. **All the structure is at the interface.** The Mandelbrot set's interior (total containment) and exterior (total escape) are featureless; the infinite detail is the boundary layer, the points that probe escape forever without leaving. Biology states the same law in the cell membrane: no wall, no charge difference; no charge difference, no energy currency; the current through the gate is the work. [empirical as mathematics and biology; structural as the general claim]
6. **Faking is a gate fault.** Expression severed from what is actually felt and valued, produced under gate pressure: current forced through a channel it is not congruent with. Its cost is paid in the record, which fills with decisions the operator did not make. (Performance itself is neutral — a craft word, judged by whether it aligns with the performer's values and whether the audience consented. Faking is the corrosive pole.)
